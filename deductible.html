Deductible break-even calculator

A higher deductible lowers your premium. This calculator shows how many claim-free years it takes for the premium savings to cover the extra amount you'd pay if you file a claim.

The formula

break-even years = (higher deductible − current deductible) ÷ yearly savings

With the example numbers: ($1,000 − $500) ÷ $150 ≈ 3.3 years. If you usually go longer than that between claims, the higher deductible saves money over time.

What the math leaves out

  • Cash on hand. Only choose a deductible you could pay tomorrow without borrowing.
  • Small claims. Many people avoid filing small claims anyway because claims can affect future premiums. That makes a higher deductible look better.
  • Percentage deductibles. Some home policies set wind or hurricane deductibles as a percentage of the dwelling limit. A 2% deductible on a $400,000 limit is $8,000. Enter that dollar amount above.
