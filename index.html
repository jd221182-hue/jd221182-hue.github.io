Open Money Calculators

Small, transparent calculators for insurance and money decisions. The formula is always shown.

Calculators

Every calculator shows its formula and runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you enter is sent anywhere.

  • Deductible break-even calculator
    Is a higher insurance deductible worth the premium savings? Calculate how many claim-free years it takes to come out ahead.
  • Mortgage points break-even calculator
    Calculate how many months it takes for mortgage discount points to pay for themselves through a lower monthly payment.
  • Should you drop collision coverage? Calculator
    Compare what collision and comprehensive cost each year to the most the insurer would ever pay on an older car.
  • Money-weighted return (XIRR) calculator
    Calculate your real annualized return when you added money at different times, the same way a spreadsheet XIRR function does.
  • Emergency fund calculator (bare-bones month)
    Size an emergency fund from the costs you'd still have to pay after losing income, not your normal spending.
