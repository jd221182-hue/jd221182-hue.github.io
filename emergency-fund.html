Emergency fund calculator

The usual advice is three to six months of expenses. The more useful number is your bare-bones month: only what you'd still have to pay if your income stopped.

How to choose the number of months

  • Lean higher with irregular or commission income, a single earner, or a long job search in your field.
  • Make sure the fund also covers your largest insurance deductible or health plan out-of-pocket maximum.
  • Keep it somewhere safe and quick to reach, like a high-yield savings account, not in stocks.

Why the bare-bones month, not normal spending

Normal spending includes things you'd cut quickly in an emergency: dining out, travel, subscriptions, new clothes, and retirement contributions. Basing the fund on normal spending can make the target feel unreachable. Basing it on the bare-bones month gives a smaller, more honest number.

Do add costs that would appear only in an emergency. The most important is health insurance: if you'd lose employer coverage with your job, the cost of continuation coverage or a marketplace plan can be several hundred dollars a month or more.

Picking the number of months

Your situation A reasonable starting point
Two stable incomes 3 months
One stable income 4 to 6 months
Variable or commission income 6 to 9 months
Self-employed 6 to 12 months
Single earner with dependents 6+ months

Building it without stalling

Most people don't fund the full amount at once. A staged approach keeps progress visible:

  1. Starter fund: enough for your largest insurance deductible.
  2. One bare-bones month.
  3. Three bare-bones months.
  4. Your full target.

Where to keep it

A high-yield savings account or money market account at a federally insured institution is the usual choice: safe, liquid, and separate from everyday checking. Avoid keeping an emergency fund in stocks, where it could be down sharply right when you need it.

Using it, then refilling it

The fund exists to be used. When you draw on it, set up an automatic transfer to rebuild it, and treat the refill like any other monthly bill until the balance is back to target.
