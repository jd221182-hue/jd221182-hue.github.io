Emergency fund calculator

The usual advice is three to six months of expenses. The more useful number is your bare-bones month: only what you'd still have to pay if your income stopped.

How to choose the number of months

  • Lean higher with irregular or commission income, a single earner, or a long job search in your field.
  • Make sure the fund also covers your largest insurance deductible or health plan out-of-pocket maximum.
  • Keep it somewhere safe and quick to reach, like a high-yield savings account, not in stocks.
